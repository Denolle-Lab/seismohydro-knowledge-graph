## Why subfields and topics fall short

## Read from OpenAlex documentation, not rerun

Read on 2026-11-03 from `help.openalex.org`.

- Prices per 1,000 calls: list $0.10, search $1, content download $10. Single-entity gets are free.
- A free key raises the daily budget from $0.10 to $1 and is required to follow a cursor past the first page. `per_page` tops out at 100.
- A new account's first $1 is paced at $0.10 per 5 minutes.
- The public snapshot is quarterly, free and anonymous on S3, about 770 GB in Parquet. Corpus-sized cursor paging is below the scale at which OpenAlex asks people to use the snapshot, about 0.3% of the roughly 327 million default-corpus works.
- OpenAlex metadata is CC0. Plaintext abstracts are not distributed, for legal reasons.
- Topics: 4 domains, 26 fields, 252 subfields, 4,516 topics. Each work gets up to three.
